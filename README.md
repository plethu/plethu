I'm Allie (plethu online). I mostly build opinionated developer tooling, as well as the open formats underneath. I do it for sheer love of the game ([and for a living when
someone's hiring](https://codeberg.org/plethu/cv)).

I care about software that's accessible, ergonomic, and trustworthy, and try to make things a little easier for creatives and everyday users. Open formats are part of that: what I put into the commons stays forkable, not stuck in a product that'll be made worse over time.

## Published

[**recite**](https://codeberg.org/plethu/recite) — A compiler, runtime, and toolchain for authoring interactive narratives. Localisable and predictable, with editor tooling out of the box. *(Pre-release.)*

[**gatekeep**](https://codeberg.org/plethu/gatekeep) — A code-first authorization engine for Rust. Every call returns the decision and a structured trace of the policy facts that produced it.

[**keepsake**](https://codeberg.org/plethu/keepsake) — A store for relation lifecycle state (holds, mutes, risk flags) with typed audit records.

‿︵‿︵‿︵‿

## In progress

**tend** — A local-first, developer-focused issue tracker designed for human-in-the-loop agent workflows.

**wardmote** — A secure local runtime for AI agent artifacts (skills, MCPs, subagent profiles). Capabilities are shared without integrity checks or permission models today; wardmote treats them as proper packages, with validation, access control, and tamper-evident audit records.
